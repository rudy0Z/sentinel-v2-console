## 2. Executive verdict

On Apple-ADA craft the foundations are genuinely disciplined — 45/45 verification gates, strict-tsc clean, a token and z-index system that held under adversarial probing, honest degraded states, and a provenance vocabulary the map itself never betrays — but the craft fails on exactly the controls that commit decisions: the focus ring is cascade-suppressed on the Authorize/Reject rows and the dissent Record button (F-14), numerals render in the proportional face against the project's own tabular ruling (F-24), and nine map-annotation selectors plus every docked drone HUD sit below the project's own 10.5px floor (F-20–F-23). On NASA/MIL-STD-1472H human-error resilience the audit found the failures that matter most: the dissent box converts operator prose into commands (clock start, mic mute, review entry that silently destroys the draft — F-01), the record-loop round trip erases committed decisions including an authorized evacuation order while the bookmark promises "the live incident stays put" (F-02), and the road-cut card asserts a modeled projection as measured ground truth under a fabricated `mea` badge with a phantom "order already approved" for 26 scene-minutes before the cut exists (F-03/F-04) — false ground truth, mode errors, and erased authorizations on life-safety paths, precisely the error classes that standard exists to design out. Tactical geospatial clarity partially held — the 3D canvas honestly labels "PROJECTED CUT · T+1:55", re-derives history exactly under scrubbing, yields labels gracefully under density, and keeps the night canvas legible — but the plan-state ladder loses all redundant encoding on draped lines (ΔE2000 ≈ 9.7 proposed-vs-committed, F-05), the camera safe frame never reserves the 372px decision lane (F-08), and the decisive "Road cut" marker is buried under the feed wing in review while the dock reads "clear · no active plan" during an executing mandatory evacuation (F-09/F-29).

### P0 — decision-integrity failures (4)

#### **[P0 · V3+V9+WALK] F-01 — Dissent box is a typing trap: opens without textarea focus (30ms focus race) and in-field Q/W/E interception + focus jump turn the operator's words into commands (clock start, mic, review, chip spam)**

- **Location & Trigger:** `commandConsole.ts:710-714` (dissent box markup — no autofocus on `#dissentText`), `:1956` (focus handed to the textarea via a 30ms `setTimeout`, racing the rAF-scheduled renderCards that creates it), `:2700-2706` (in-field empty-note Q/W/E branch; focus handed to the Record button at `:2704`) interacting with the global single-key branch `:2716-2764` (Space `:2735-2737`, digits `:2716-2727`, `r` review `:2742`, `m` mic `:2744-2751`, T/I panel/thermal) and global chip append `:2757-2763` (no preventDefault, refocuses the textarea); `setReview` clears the draft at `:2067`. Bites in phase D/E while answering a gate card and typing a reason.
- **Cognitive Failure Mode:** The dissent box is a typing trap with two coupled mechanisms. (1) Opening it leaves focus on BODY (or a button): the deferred focus is a 30ms timer racing an unsynchronized rAF render — intermittent by construction — and the markup has no autofocus, so the natural "start typing my reason" flow never reaches `#dissentText`: typed characters are dropped or fire global single-key triggers (Space = play/pause clock, M = mic, T = panel, I = thermal, R = review which destroys the draft at `:2067`, 1-9 answer the focus card). (2) While `#dissentText` is focused with an empty note, keys q/w/e are intercepted even when the operator is starting a sentence; the first hit inserts a doctrinal chip AND moves focus to the Record button, so every remaining prose keystroke lands on a non-input target and fires global single-key actions, and the global chip branch keeps appending chips whenever focus is outside the field. Typing inside a focused textarea is fully protected (verified) — the guard works; the flow just never gets you there and does not keep you there. Norman capture/mode slip under stress: an operator drafting "we should stage first" gets chips injected mid-word, their prose's space restarts the exercise clock from the paused statutory hold (`:2735`; lapse receipt "Nothing was sent.", `talon.ts:378-379` — plan gate hold 7.8, `gate.expires` 16, `data/scenarios/wui-breakout.json`), an `r` drops the console into read-only review while the operator believes they are typing a note, M mutes the mic mid-sentence; a natural-typing probe left the note EMPTY, and a recorded dissent read "Field / radio intel supersedes the projection. Crew or aircraft safety margin.words: hold for recon" — chips spliced mid-sentence with the typed leading words lost, no visible cue that focus never entered the textarea. One confirmer found a stronger consequence: a stray `1` from stranded focus committed the recommended option — it authorized Plan P-1 while the operator believed they were typing a rejection.
- **Senior Design Recommendation:** Focus `#dissentText` the moment `ui.dissent` is set, from the render commit rather than a timer (end of the renderCards branch that builds the dissent box, or chain the existing `setTimeout` behind a double `requestAnimationFrame`) on both the click path (`:1952-1953`) and the keyboard path; restrict chips to pointer clicks plus the global (non-typing-context) Q/W/E branch only — if the glove path must keep in-field keys, intercept only when the textarea has received no input event in this draft instance and return focus to the textarea after insertion instead of (or in addition to) the Record button (submit stays Ctrl+Enter, `:2707`); suppress global single-key actions while `ui.dissent` is open (or route printable characters into the textarea when it is open and focus is on a non-input element). Esc/Ctrl+Enter behavior is unaffected (Esc already works while typing, verified).
- **Evidence:** Probe (`.v2c/probe-v3v9.mjs`, `?situation=wui-breakout&boot=live`, t=10): clicking "2 Reject plan" opened dissent; typing "wind shifted north; spread model stale" produced `#dissentText.value` = "Crew or aircraft safety margin. Local terrain or fuel factor.ed north; spread model stale" — the W/E chip strings (`DISSENT_CHIPS`, `commandConsole.ts:207-211`) injected by the prose letters, only the tail typed — and scene time advanced 10 → 10.428 with no deliberate Space press. Control probe (`.v2c/probe-clock.mjs`): idle t and option-click-only t stay frozen at 10 for 2.5s, so the clock start can only have come from a prose space hitting the global Space branch. Focus race: `.v2c/walk-injects/probe2-run.log` "E2 dissent box state" = `{focused:''…}` and "E3 after Q" = "Field / radio intel supersedes the projection.q" (global path took the keystroke); `debug3.mjs` pre-Esc `{focusId:'dissentText'}` — same environment, intermittent. `.v2c/walk-breakui/kb2.json` trapState/trapDetail — note empty after natural typing (kb2-typing-trap.png); `kb-walk.json` dissentWords mangling (kb-dissent-submitted.png); typeGuard — typing INSIDE the focused textarea fully protected. Code read: `commandConsole.ts:710-714, 2062-2074, 2698-2708, 2757-2765`.
- **Status:** **verified** — independently confirmed by code read (compiled `commandConsole.js` in sync at :2052/:2843-2922/:717) plus live headless probes (ports 8146-8150): 21 focus-race cycles — focus landed in the textarea 0/20 times when frames were slow (measured rAF gaps 5–759ms under SwiftShader); natural typing destroyed prose and started the clock (10 → 10.258); 6/6 keyboard-path typing attempts lost the note (mic muted 3/6, review opened 2/6); the corrupted record reproduced byte-for-byte; typing inside the focused textarea landed 100% with zero side effects. Corrections folded in: the plan gate's `expires` is 16 (not 8); the bite is on the phase-D gate card (phase E cannot start pre-authorization).
- **Ruling:** `new` — a defect in the shipped dissent ritual, not the ritual itself: the dissent box/placeholder wording is deliberate (restraint-register ruling; DESIGN.md §4.4 "Dissent is preserved"); the focus race and Q/W/E interception are implementation defects. Code anchor verified: `commandConsole.ts:710-714` markup, `:1956` 30ms focus timer, `:2700-2706` in-field interception.
