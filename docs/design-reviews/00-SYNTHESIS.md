# 00 · Synthesis — cross-lens design review of the Sentinel command console

Synthesized 2026-11-01 from the ten lens reports in this directory (`01-taste-skill.md` … `10-break-ui.md`), each read in full this session. Convergence across independent lenses is treated as signal; single-lens findings are labelled as such; disagreements are stated, not smoothed over.

**Method & verification.** This file aggregates; it does not re-judge. No lens suite was re-executed by the synthesizer. The checks I ran this session are three spot-checks of the most load-bearing cross-lens claims, all of which confirmed what the reports state:

1. `grep -n "sitbtn" console.css` → `min-width: 0` on `.sitbtn` (console.css:428) and ellipsis on `.sitbtn b`/`.sitbtn small` (console.css:430-431); **no `.sitbtn > span` rule exists anywhere** — confirming the root cause four lenses trace (04 §P0-1, 05 §P1-1, 06 §P1-1, 07 §P0-2).
2. `awk 'NR>1124' console.css | grep -cE '#[0-9A-Fa-f]{3,8}|rgba?\('` → **9 lines** of raw literals at console.css:1127–1141, matching better-colors §P1 ("9 offending lines"; hallmark counts 12 *literals* because line 1127's shadow stacks four rgba values — same section, consistent counts).
3. `grep -c "::selection\|caret-color" console.css` → **0**, confirming impeccable §P1-3 and hallmark §F16.

Scores, verdicts, severities and check attributions below are each lens's own, as written in its report. Where a lens ran something notable (hallmark ran `node tools/verify.mjs` → 44/44 passed and a live sloplint render; better-ui ran live headless-Chrome geometry probes), that is stated inside the relevant report and repeated here with attribution.

## (2) Convergent findings — flagged independently by 3+ lenses

### C1 · Topbar cells overprint at the 1280×800 verified floor — **8 lenses** (strongest signal in the review set)
01 §P0-1, 02 §P1-1, 04 §P0-1, 05 §P1-1, 06 §P1-1, 07 §P0-2, 09 §P0-1, 10 §P1-3.
Eight lenses, working from the same `qa/e2e-break-1280.png`, each independently recorded the incident title painting through the posture pill, gate chip and TFR chip. Four (04, 05, 06, 07) traced the identical mechanism: `.sitbtn` carries `min-width: 0` (console.css:428) but its inner `<span>` (rendered at `commandConsole.ts:542-543`) does not, so the nowrap title escapes the shrinking button — my spot-check confirms no `.sitbtn > span` rule exists. 05 notes the occluded element includes the statutory gate countdown; 09 notes the collision occurs *while the ≤1310 compact tier is visibly active*, contradicting the guard's own comment ("before the posture pill can ever collide", console.css:995-997). Seven lenses converge on the same one-line fix plus two reinforcements: `.sitbtn > span { min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; }` (04, 05, 06, 07), a compact/short-form TFR chip at ≤1310px (01, 04, 06, 09), and an e2e bounding-box no-intersection guard at 1280 phase D (01, 02, 05, 06, 07, 09, 10). On whether the shipped PNG reflects HEAD, the lenses disagree — see §(4) D1.

### C2 · Dock overflow affordance missing/unguarded at intermediate widths — **7 lenses**
01 §P2-6, 02 §P2-4, 04 §P1-2, 05 §P1-3, 07 §P1-3, 09 §P1-3, 10 §P1-1 (06 §P2-8 raises the same class at 1920 but flags it "could not reproduce").
The dock guard matrix covers only 1280 (pill must exist) and 1920 (no overflow) — DESIGN.md §7 guard 3 — leaving 1440, 1600, 1680 unpinned. 10 computed the five-widget strip at 1748px (widths from console.css:752-756), so it clips at every viewport under 1772px. 07's live probe found a worse mode: after `player.setTime(6)` or a 1px resize round-trip, 2 widgets clip and the pill is absent — `updateDockMore` is only called from `renderSlots` (`commandConsole.ts:1085`). Four lenses (02, 04, 09, 10) independently note the 1440 evidence (`qa/final-rev.png`, 2026-10-27) predates the dock-more commits, so the current 1440 state is unverified — a shared stale-evidence caveat, not a disagreement about the guard gap.

### C3 · Map tool column shares the cards/chat lane; tools render behind or click-dead under open cards — **4 lenses**
02 §P2-6, 05 §P1-2, 07 §P0-1, 09 §P0-2.
`.tools` and `.cards`/`.chat` share the identical `right: calc(var(--gap) * 2 + var(--rail-w2))` anchor (console.css:452, 530, 547), with tools at z10 under cards at z30. 07 verified live: 42×302px overlap at 1280 and 1920, 42×117px at 1440, and `elementFromPoint` shows all seven tool buttons covered by card children — "the operator loses map navigation exactly when a decision card is open." 09 found the same failure in two of the four shipped captures (only the bottommost two icons clear the card in `qa/calm-plan.png`) and notes the anatomy sheets draw both anchors "without resolving their overlap" (01-shell.html:164-183). 02 and 05 flag the sibling case: the ALSO WAITING queue's countdown and kbd hints run under the zoom tools and rail, and the e2e V1 clearance check covers `#cards` but never the queue.

### C4 · Fire growth chart's x-axis labels sliced/faded at the widget bottom — **3 lenses**
01 §P1-2, 02 §P1-2, 04 §P1-1.
01 attributes it to the always-on 32px `.fade-y` mask (console.css:935-940, 960-964, `--fade-len` :240); 02 and 04 attribute it to hard clipping — axis `<text>` at the SVG's bottom edge (`commandConsole.ts:2450`) inside a 204px `.widget { overflow: hidden }` well (console.css:751). The mechanisms are complementary (both the fade and the clip land on the same labels), and all three agree on the defect and its persistence: 04 verified it in the current build via `qa/e2e-break-cards.png` (2026-10-30). 02 adds adjacent tick labels colliding ("+30 m" over "T+1:1…"). The fix converges on reserving an axis band inside the widget body (01, 02, 04).

### C5 · Self-documented concentric-radius conflict on coa/dissent/menu — **6 lenses**
01 §P2-7i, 02 §P2-7, 03 §F15, 04 §P2-3, 05 §P2-1, 07 §P2-7.
The anatomy self-records it: "CONFLICT: applied inner radius on coa/dissent/menu is 4 where the formula yields 2" (docs/anatomy/index.html:108). The CSS comments encode the wrong arithmetic ("10px pop − 12px padding → 4px", console.css:470, 654, 673 — `max(2, 10−12)` is 2). Six lenses flag it; they split on the resolution: 02 and 07 say keep the applied 4px and fix the comments/formula, while 04 and 05 present apply-2-or-amend as equally open. Cosmetic (2px) but on the focal card — 04 calls it "the 'most exacting designer screenshots the one wrong thing' class of fault."

### C6 · Zero-raw-hex contract drift — raw literals outside the token blocks — **3 lenses**
02 §P2-10, 03 §P0-2, 08 §P1.

## (4) Where lenses disagree, and why

**D1 · Is the 1280 topbar break live, or a stale capture?** 07 ran a fresh headless capture and reports it "a pixel match for the shipped `qa/e2e-break-1280.png` garble — the break captured 2026-10-27 … is **still live**" (§P0-2). 10 reasons from the CSS that "a static flex row cannot overprint, so this frame is most plausibly a pre-fix build," while conceding "the collision class remains reachable in current code" (§P1-3). 09 states the mechanism "is not determinable from the static capture — live repro at HEAD: **Not verified**" (§P0-1). These are compatible on the mechanism (four lenses traced the identical missing `min-width: 0`) — the disagreement is strictly about whether the shipped PNG reflects HEAD, and 07's live probe is the strongest evidence on that question. The disagreement matters because it decides whether the fix is a code change, an evidence re-shoot, or both (07's probe implies both).

**D2 · Tracked-caps labels: earned identity or the largest machine-tell?** 03 §P0-1 ranks the systemic tracked-caps voice as the top finding — "the single largest machine-tell on the screen" under an unwaivable house Floor — while itself conceding "a tactical-console owner could reasonably argue it." 01 §P2-7d judges the same idiom "defensible in kind": "each caps label carries a value or an authority state. Keep — but this is the budget the console most clearly exceeds." 02 records the equivalent caps-meta-row as "judged earned in Operate mode" against its ban's absolute wording. Same device, opposite calls — the split tracks whether the lens weights genre convention (tactical-console authenticity) or mechanical anti-slop enforcement, and 03 honestly marks it "a DESIGN.md amendment to negotiate with the rubric, not a silent keep."

**D3 · Chrome-overlap: interaction defect or frame-selection matter?** 07 §P0-1 and 09 §P0-2 grade the tools-behind-cards state HIGH/Block — an operator cannot navigate the map during a decision. 04 §P2-1 addresses the sibling case (card column occluding the map's wind readout in stills) and rules it "Not a product bug — a frame-selection rule," recommending a hero-crop checklist instead of a code change. The two positions are about different elements (clickable tool column vs. passive readout), but they grade the same overlap class oppositely, and 04 never assessed the click-dead case. For product code, 07/09's call governs; 04's caveat is legitimately scoped to marketing stills.
