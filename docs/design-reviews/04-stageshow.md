## (a) Lens summary

Stageshow builds product UI that must survive as a single frame on a marketing page. Its one idea: *"It reads as a real product, simplified to what the viewer needs, framed with intent, and every motion has a cause."* It fails five ways — **too much** (real text packed everywhere reads generated), **wireframe**, **boxed** (a whole window pasted with even margins), **flat** (no place for the eye to land), **bland**. The fix is a full, structured screen in quiet grays, real words only where the story is, a crop that puts the eye on the payoff. Its recurring fault list doubles as hard gates: content that swaps instead of moving; gray bars where the eye lands; a plate behind the window or air on four sides; **empty bands, cut controls**; stock AI (sparkles, purple) and made-up logos; busy rows and uneven insets; long holds and causeless resets. Fidelity is graded — `realistic` (real content everywhere) is correct at hero scale for a named product, so density is licensed; what is never licensed is a sliced string, a dead corner, or a frame where the eye lands anywhere but the result. Motion judgment: posters never show a caught mid-change; loops reset with a visible cause; exits are faster than entrances.

## (b) Verdict + score

**8 / 10** (the skill defines no numeric scale; 1–10 per the ask). Believability is near-perfect — this reads as a real product at realistic fidelity better than most marketing mockups — but every provided still below 1920 contains at least one hard-sliced string, including a three-string overprint at the console's own verified floor, and a sliced string is precisely the fault a stageshow frame cannot carry.

## (c) Findings

### P0-1 · Topbar overprint at the verified floor — three strings paint over each other

**Evidence.** `qa/e2e-break-1280.png`, top-left (verified at 3×, crop 0,0→760,70): the incident title *"Encinal Canyon · WUI breakout"*, the `PLAN` posture pill and the amber `GATE 10:00 LEFT` chip are superimposed into unreadable noise, with the TFR chip *"TFR · AIR ATTACK OPS · UAS FLOOR < 150′"* beside them. The console's own CSS names this exact hazard: *"1280px floor: compact the topbar **before the posture pill can ever collide** with the situation meta"* (`console.css:995-997`, the ≤1310 compact block at `console.css:998-1001` hides `.sitbtn small` and KPI cells). The guard exists and does not hold.
**Mechanism (traced, not guessed).** Cell order brand · sitbtn · posture · tfrPill… (`commandConsole.ts:541-547`); the sitbtn body is `<span><b>…</b><small>…</small></span>` (`commandConsole.ts:542`). `.sitbtn` carries `min-width: 0` (`console.css:428`) and `.sitbtn b`/`small` carry ellipsis (`console.css:430-431`), but the inner `<span>` wrapper has **no** `min-width: 0`, so it keeps content width; `.sitbtn` shrinks while its text spills beneath the later-painted, `white-space: nowrap` `.posture`/`.gatechip` (`console.css:537`, `.gatechip` rule follows at 543). Sheet-02 note 2 states the contract the implementation misses: "MIN-WIDTH 0 + ELLIPSIS ON TEXT BLOCKS; ROW NEVER SCROLLS" (`docs/anatomy/02-topbar.html:136`).
**Controlled comparison.** Same build, same run, 1600×960: `qa/e2e-break-cards.png` shows the topbar clean — title, PLAN + GATE chip, TFR pill separated. The fault is width-specific at 1280 in the current build.
**Fix (buildable).** One line: `.sitbtn > span { min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; }` — ellipsis then engages exactly as sheet-02 note 2 requires. Additionally, the always-rendered TFR chip (`commandConsole.ts:533-536` renders it in every mode, ~250 px) is not in the ≤1310 compact budget; at ≤1310 collapse it to a short `TFR` chip (its full text already lives in the `title` attribute). Frame rule: a 1280 capture cannot be a hero until this is fixed.

### P1-1 · Fire growth axis labels sliced mid-glyph — in the poster frame, and persisting in the newest build

**Evidence.** `qa/final-polish.png` bottom dock (verified at 3×, crop 330,845→720,900): the chart's x-axis row (*"T+0:04 / 15 min / 30 min / T+1:13 / T+2:2…"*) is sliced horizontally through the glyphs by the widget's bottom edge — crisp cut, no fade. Also visible in `qa/final-rev.png` (right-edge crop) and `qa/calm-plan.png` (bottom-right crop). Crucially it **persists in the current build**: `qa/e2e-break-cards.png` (2026-10-30) shows the same sliced glyph tops along the Fire growth widget's bottom edge at 1600×960 (crop 1020,870→1400,955).
**Mechanism.** `.widget` has a fixed `height: var(--slots-h)` (204 px; 172 ≤780 h) with `overflow: hidden` (`console.css:751`); the growth chart renders axis `<text class="axis" y="${H - 3}">` at the SVG's bottom edge (`commandConsole.ts:2450`) and the legend row adds height below (`commandConsole.ts:1172`), so the axis row lands outside the 204 px well and is hard-clipped. The documented 32 px fade mask (`--fade-len: 32px`, `console.css:240`; masks at `console.css:935-945`) never reaches the cut.
**Fix (buildable).** Reserve a footer band inside the 204 px budget: render axis labels at `y = H − 12` with a matching SVG bottom inset, or move the axis row into a fixed-height HTML footer (like the header's 38 px) so nothing renders at the clip line; alternatively let the chart body scroll under the existing `.wbody` fade so an overflow fades instead of slicing. The poster frame must contain no sliced string — this one sits in the frame a marketing page would ship.

### P1-2 · Overflowing dock strip slices crisp at the viewport edge; affordance unguarded at 1440

**Evidence.** `qa/final-rev.png` right edge (verified at 3×, crop 1290,655→1440,900): the 5th widget (*"Fire gro…" / "206 ac"*) is hard-cropped at the viewport with **no** `.dock-more` pill (the crop covers the pill's documented anchor) and **no** fade — while `qa/e2e-break-1280.png` shows the pill at 1280 (*"+2 widgets ▸"*) and `qa/e2e-break-cards.png` shows *"+1 widget ▸"* at 1600. DESIGN.md §7 guard 3 asserts the affordance at 1280 and its absence at 1920 only — **1440 is an unguarded mid-state**. Caveat stated plainly: `final-rev.png` is dated 2026-10-27 and may predate the current dock-more build; the affordance-gap at 1440 is evidenced only by that still, while the crisp-slice-with-no-fade part is current (visible at 1600 too).
**Mechanism.** `.slots` is the masked scroll container (`console.css:742`, mask `console.css:941-945`, `fade-x-run` keyframes `console.css:972-977` — at scroll 0 the 0 % keyframe sets `--fade-end: calc(100% − var(--fade-len))`, i.e. the overflowing right edge is *supposed* to fade 32 px). In all four stills the cut is crisp: the mask is not reading on the visible edge.
**Fix (buildable).** (1) Extend the e2e dock guard to loop every test width where `scrollWidth > clientWidth` (1280/1440/1600) asserting the pill; (2) make the fade provable — add a guard that samples the last 32 px of `.slots` for non-zero alpha, and if the scroll-timeline mask still fails, set the static `@supports not (animation-timeline: scroll())` fallback mask (`console.css:986-991`) on `.slots` via a TS-toggled `is-overflowing` class; (3) for marketing captures, shoot at 1920 (`calm-plan.png`'s dock row is clean) or crop before the last widget.

### P1-3 · Unscrolled overflowing card stack slices the focal card's last line

**Evidence.** `qa/calm-plan.png` (1920×1080), EGRESS · PROPOSAL card — the frame's focal object (top-right, tallest, amber `10:00 left`): the model note *"TALON egress model · road capacity 14 veh/min one lane · 1.6 vehicles per home"* is sliced mid-glyph at the card's bottom edge, and the card's `Why? / Show on map` row is cut off entirely (verified at 2×, crop 1480,700→1900,800). Same card renders cleanly in the current build at 1600 when the COA compare is collapsed (`qa/e2e-break-cards.png`); the sliced state is triggered by the expanded COA table overflowing the stack's max height.
**Mechanism.** `.cards` clips at `max-height: calc(100vh − topbar − slots − 44px)` with `overflow-y: auto` (`console.css:547`; anatomy `01-shell.html` CARDS-MH row). The stack's fade variant deliberately skips fades when unscrolled: *"#cards is a content-height stack: skip fades until it actually scrolls so a short stack's last card isn't dimmed"* (`console.css:956-958`), and `fade-y-run-content`'s 0 % keyframe is `--fade-start: 0%; --fade-end: 100%` — no fade at all (`console.css:966-971`). Cost of that opt-out: an overflowing, unscrolled stack slices its last card crisp, with no affordance that there is more.
**Fix (buildable).** When the stack overflows at scroll 0 the bottom must fade, not slice: have the TS (which already measures `--dock-h`) toggle an `is-overflowing` class on `#cards` when `scrollHeight > clientHeight`, applying the static bottom-fade mask; or clamp the card's basis text (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2` on `.basis`) so the card never ends on a cut line. A hero frame whose payoff card ends in a sliced sentence fails the lens outright.

### P2-1 · Map readouts occluded mid-glyph by the decision card (still-selection hazard)

**Evidence.** `qa/final-polish.png` (verified 3×, crop 850,65→1040,135): the map's weather pill *"WIND ENE / RH 8% · 6…°F"* is sliced mid-content by the focal card's left edge. Also `qa/calm-plan.png` (the *"+60 min"* chip and *"ROUTE B — MULHOLLAND RWY"* run under the card) and `qa/e2e-break-1280.png` (*"…TION / T-0:16"* annotation at the card edge). Floating chrome over a full-bleed map is legitimate live behavior; in a still it reads as a cut control.
**Fix.** Not a product bug — a frame-selection rule: the capture mode used for marketing stills should pick a T where the card column does not straddle the wind readout (the pill sits at a fixed anchor; the card column is 68 px from the right edge per anatomy `01-shell.html` Detail A), or nudge map readouts with the same collision-aware anchoring the tool groups have. Document a "hero crop checklist" next to the QA captures.

### P2-2 · `.dock-more` pill sits inside the last widget's header band

**Evidence.** `qa/e2e-break-1280.png` (verified 3×, crop 990,555→1280,625): the *"+2 widgets ▸"* chip overlaps the Fire growth widget's header area, ellipsizing *"TALO…"* right where it lands. Its anchor is `right: var(--s-1); bottom: calc(100% − var(--s-4))` (`console.css:747`) — 16 px into the dock, i.e. fully inside the 38 px widget header band (anatomy index, Control heights row). The straddle itself is the lens's own corner-chip pattern (gold `corner-chip`: a chip centered on a corner, half outside) — it is just anchored one band too low.
**Fix.** `bottom: calc(100% − 6px)` so the pill straddles the dock's top edge line instead of covering header content.

### P2-3 · Concentric-radius CONFLICT on coa/dissent/menu (documented in the anatomy)

**Evidence.** `docs/anatomy/index.html:108` — "CONFLICT: applied inner radius on coa/dissent/menu is 4 where the formula yields 2" (concentric rule `r_in = max(2, r_out − pad)`). Visible territory: the COA compare table inside the calm-plan hero card. This is the "most exacting designer screenshots the one wrong thing" class of fault — 2 px, but on the focal card.
**Fix.** Set the inner radius on `.coa`, `.dissent` and menus to `max(2px, calc(var(--r-lg) − var(--s-3)))` so the documented formula holds.

### Documented-deliberate conflict · violet on the focal card vs the lens's "no purple" ban

The lens bans purple as the stock-AI tell. The console's focal decision cards carry violet evidence chips — *"9 homes in 2h"*, *"E-32"* (`qa/final-polish.png`), *"Zone 1 / Route A / Hairpin"* (`qa/calm-plan.png`) — via `.chip.talon` (`console.css:403`) plus the violet provenance square before TALON widget titles (`console.css:797`, visible on "Fire growth"/"Structures at risk" headers in the crops). DESIGN.md §5 documents violet = TALON activity as constitutional (red discipline: red = life safety, amber = waiting on human). **Reported as a conflict, not a fault**: the semantic is enforced by the verify guard and defensible. But note the marketing consequence: in the poster frame the second-loudest accent after the amber countdown is purple chips — exactly the pattern the lens flags. If hero frames are curated, prefer frames where amber leads and violet stays small; do not remove the violet.

## (d) What the lens would genuinely praise

- **Believability.** Real units everywhere (veh/min, m/min, DBM, AGL, ERC, WEA device counts, reverse-911 line counts), consistent callsigns across map, widgets and cards (FT-01…03, E-31/32, SH-12, DZ-3), T+ scene time aligned with a wall clock and PST, provenance badges (MEA). Microcopy is grounded in code, not decoration: *"Waits for you"* is literally the `authority === 'operator'` branch (`commandConsole.ts:708`); *"Why?"* / *"Show on map"* are real actions (`commandConsole.ts:709`). Nothing reads generated — the "reads as a real product" bar is cleared at full realistic fidelity.
- **Un-boxed framing.** Floating chrome over a full-bleed map: no plate, no air on four sides, and no dead corners — the 1920 bottom-right that the dock does not fill is carried by the fire perimeter and structure data (verified crop `br-corner`), which is exactly how the lens wants leftovers handled.
- **Hierarchy with a landing spot.** The eye goes to the decision card and its amber countdown; the near-mono terrain stays quiet behind it. One window clearly leads in every still.
- **Native use of the lens's own patterns.** The *"ALSO WAITING"* card stacked behind the focal card (`qa/final-polish.png`) is the stacked-cards/satellite pattern occurring in-product; the amber "2" badge straddling the rail warning button (`final-polish.png`, verified 4×) is `corner-chip` done right; the truncated, pinned *"ALSO WAITING"* follower is depth without ornament.
- **Texture judgment.** One glass recipe for all chrome with a 4.5 % film grain that "kills gradient banding and reads as sensor texture" (`console.css:366-367`) — precisely the right call at hero scale.
- **Restraint written into CSS.** "One accent, never a full coloured frame: a 2px edge cue, not an alarm box" (`console.css:604-608`) with corner ticks on hover (`console.css:610-621`) is the lens's quiet-hierarchy rule as code.
- **Motion contract, on paper.** `--ease-out-expo` damped release, entrances 120 ms / exits 80 ms, compositor-only `scaleX` bars, and — rare — a reduced-motion block that names the exact looping classes it stops (`.dot.working`, `.live::before`, the mic, the orb parts; `console.css:328-341`, DESIGN.md §6). The anatomy's motion row (`docs/anatomy/index.html:113`) matches the CSS. Replay is an explicit scrub surface with its own chrome, so "data never rewinds without a cause" is satisfied by design.
