Why it looks like this

  • Both languages on one alert. The resident does not change a setting at 03:53. The requisition carries EN+ES copy because the geofence cannot know the SIM language.
  • The geofence check travels with the message. "Your neighbor outside the zone did not [receive this]" is the console's spill check, made legible to the public — the same polygon check the operator saw on the gate.
  • Issuer vocabulary belongs to the issuer. The console requisitioned; the alert says Evacuation Order because the Sheriff / County OES issued it. Sentinel's own surfaces say "requisition" — this artifact is the one place the word "order" is correct.
  • It cannot be silenced. The same statutory weight the operator's gate carried — mirrored at the last inch.
